    - Environmental Health (Pollution/Greenery).
    - Route Planning (Eco-friendly paths).
    - Issue Reporting (Fix My Street integration).
- **GovHub:** A comprehensive portal for:
    - Official Services.
    - AI-Powered Support (SmartGov).
    - Policy Simulation (GovSimulator).
- **EcoSouq:** A single marketplace for local and international eco-products.
