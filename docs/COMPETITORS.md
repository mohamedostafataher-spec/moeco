# Competitor analysis — MoEco (November 2026)

Scope: apps an Egyptian user might pick instead of MoEco for environmental
tracking, waste/recycling, or civic reporting. Sources linked per entry.

## Direct / Egyptian market

### Bekia (bekia-egypt.com)
- What: lets households and businesses **sell recyclable waste** (plastic, paper,
  metal, electronics) for cash or points; pickup-based collection network.
- Sources: https://bekia-egypt.com/ , https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bekia_egypt.bekia
- Strengths: real monetary incentive, physical operations, brand recognition in Cairo/Alex.
- Weaknesses vs MoEco: transactional only — no footprint calculator, no utility
  bill tools, no civic complaint organizing, no air-quality/weather awareness layer.
- Overlap: waste-to-income. MoEco's WasteToIncome page is educational; Bekia is the
  actual marketplace. We should link out honestly rather than compete.

### Dawar (dawarapp.com)
- What: circular-economy infrastructure app; citizens report and manage waste,
  with corporate/municipal clients. Originated with Ministry of Environment
  collaboration.
- Sources: https://dawarapp.com/who-we-are/ , https://www.egyptindependent.com/dawar-waste-collection-app-allows-citizens-to-manage-and-report-trash/
- Strengths: institutional backing, real collection logistics.
- Weaknesses vs MoEco: focused on waste reporting only; no personal carbon
  accounting, prices/tariffs, or daily-life utilities.
- Overlap: environmental reporting. MoEco's differentiator is organizing the
  complaint for *any* official channel, not only waste.

## Global awareness apps (indirect)

### AWorld for ActNow (actnow.aworld.org)
- What: UN ActNow campaign app; daily sustainable-habit logging and challenges.
- Source: https://actnow.aworld.org/
- Strengths: UN branding, polished habit loops, multi-language.
- Weaknesses vs MoEco: global averages — no Egyptian grid factors, EGP tariffs,
  fuel prices, prayer-time context, or Arabic-first Egyptian dialect UX.

### Commons (thecommons.earth)
- What: tracks the carbon footprint of your **spending** by linking payment
  cards (US-centric).
- Source: https://www.thecommons.earth/how-it-works
- Strengths: passive automatic tracking, strong design.
- Weaknesses vs MoEco: requires card linking (US banks), subscription-based
  features, no Egypt data. Not usable in Egypt today.

## Positioning conclusions

1. **No Egyptian app combines** personal footprint + local tariffs/prices +
   civic complaints + air quality in Arabic. MoEco's wedge is the *bundle*
   localized for Egypt, free.
2. Do not build a recyclables marketplace; link to Bekia where relevant.
3. Differentiation to keep investing in: Egyptian data auto-updating (fuel,
   electricity tiers, metro fares), Egyptian Arabic copy, local-first privacy,
   official-channel complaint organizing.
4. Watch: if Dawar or Bekia add footprint features, the moat narrows — the
   defense is data freshness and the daily-usefulness loop (prayer times,
   bills, AQ).
